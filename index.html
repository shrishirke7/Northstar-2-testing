Manual IP mapping previews the flow; secure login and user accounts are managed by your administrator.
1 · Map workstation IP

Admin registers a trusted device IP against a user, department and factory.

2 · Verify identity

Users sign in with their local application account.

3 · Open request link

Signed-in users get their mapped name, department and site prefilled in the issue form.

4 · Track to closure

IT updates assignee, status, work notes and expected completion time in the live queue.
